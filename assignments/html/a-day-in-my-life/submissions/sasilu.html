my daily routine (bare minimum edition)

i’m just going with the flow at this point

morning

wake up… eventually.
stare at ceiling for a bit questioning why i exist again.
check phone, regret it, still don’t get up.
getting out of bed feels like a side quest i never accept


late morning

finally get up but move like a glitchy npc.
brush teeth half asleep, no thoughts behind eyes.
skip breakfast sometimes because effort.
“i’ll eat later” (i do not eat later)


afternoon

try to be productive… fail immediately.
sit down, open something, close it, repeat.
scroll phone for “just 5 minutes” (it lies).
time disappears and i have no proof it existed


evening

think about doing work.
think harder.
still thinking.
somehow tired from doing nothing all day


night

lie in bed and realize i did nothing useful today again.
say “tomorrow i’ll fix my life” (i won’t).
scroll until my brain shuts off by itself.
sleep because there’s nothing else to do

summary

low energy, high procrastination, somehow still surviving.
