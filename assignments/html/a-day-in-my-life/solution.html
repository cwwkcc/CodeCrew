The Great Coffee Disaster ☕

Morning

The day started beautifully. The birds were singing, the sun was shining, and I felt completely rested for the first time in weeks. I walked into the kitchen, turned on the espresso machine, and knew that securing my morning caffeine was the most critical mission of the day. Everything was going according to plan until the cat decided to jump onto the counter.

Afternoon

By 2:00 PM, the situation had escalated. I was sitting in the library trying to finish my assignment when I reached for my travel mug. It tipped over in slow motion. I watched the dark liquid spread across my keyboard, freezing the screen instantly.
Silence fell over the room as my laptop let out one final, tragic beep.

Evening

After a trip to the repair shop, I walked home in the rain. Today taught me a harsh but valuable lesson: liquids and expensive electronics do not belong on the same table. I spent the rest of the night reading a physical, paper book by the window. At least you can't short-circuit a paperback.

Quote of the Day

"Expect the best, but prepare for the spilled coffee."

As I drifted off to sleep, feeling peaceful at last, my eyes shot open in the dark. I had left the iron on this morning.
